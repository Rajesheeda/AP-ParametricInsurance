How a decision is made

  • Weather trigger. Acute events: the event's 3-day rainfall beats the mandal's own 99th percentile (CHIRPS 1995–2024), or a cyclone ≥34 kt passes within 150 km (IBTrACS). Seasons: the WBCIS-style season stress index crosses its 1-in-5-year trigger.
  • Satellite confirmation. Acute: Sentinel-1 flooded cropland above the mandal's measured false-alarm floor, from an image within 72 h, on crops standing that day (e-Panta sowing date + FAO-56 duration). Seasons: MODIS VCI < 35 (Kogan).
  • Action. Both agree → Parametric payout. One only → Field / photo verification. Neither → No payout.
  • Management-water gate. Where e-Panta shows paddy being planted around the event date, SAR "flood" may be puddling water, so it cannot confirm loss on its own.

Pilot selection rule

Eligible = at least one qualifying event 2020–2025; ranked by focus-crop acres (e-Panta 2025); YSR also needs ≥50% parcel coverage.

Acute events: loss assessment per pilot mandal

Affected acres = crop acres standing on the event date × (flooded cropland share − SAR false-alarm floor). Mandal-level flood is spread over cropland by crop area; parcel-level mapping is available in YSR Kadapa only.

Drought seasons (Kharif 2020–2025; Rabi for Chittoor)

Only seasons whose stress came from rainfall deficit or dry spells. VCI from MODIS MOD13Q1 over cropland, scaled per mandal 2001–2025.

Trigger structure (WBCIS term-sheet form)

  • Crop season split into FAO-56 stages (initial, development, mid, late), starting from the median e-Panta sowing date of that crop in that mandal.
  • Per stage: deficit rainfall, excess (max 3-day) rainfall, dry spell (days < 2.5 mm, IMD rainy-day threshold).
  • Each value → its percentile in the mandal's own 1995–2024 CHIRPS record → standard normal score (as SPI). Stage stress = worst peril; season stress = water-demand-weighted sum (FAO-56 Kc × days).
  • Trigger = 1-in-5-year season stress; exit = 1-in-25-year. Payout rises linearly from 0 to 100% of sum insured.
  • Premium = pure burn cost (mean payout 1995–2024). Insurer loading is added on top.

    Known limits (stated up front)

    • Real CCE yields cover YSR Kadapa only (Chapad and Khajipet among 26 mandals, 2019–2025). No CCE or claims for Palnadu, Manyam or Chittoor, and no PMFBY claim amounts. Against CCE, weather triggers alone fall short of the ≥75% target; see the CCE card above.
    • Parcel boundaries exist for YSR Kadapa only (about 56% of villages); 8.5k APAIMS polygons share an ID and are excluded from parcel analysis.
    • Sentinel-1 had a 12-day repeat in 2022–2024 (one satellite); the 72-hour window is met by radar alone in under half the events.
    • SAR cannot tell disaster flooding from paddy puddling; the e-Panta planting-window gate sends those cases to field verification.
    • CHIRPS is published about a month late; September 2026 events use ERA5 and are marked provisional.
    • Irrigation source is unknown for about 70% of YSR acreage, so rainfall triggers also run over some irrigated land.
